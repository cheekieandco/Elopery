Hi, I’m Macy.

CEO of The Elopery

The Elopery is a modern elopement chapel and celebration venue coming to Alexandria, VA. Our packages are all-inclusive, which keeps planning stress-free. You pick a date and a package, and we handle the rest.

  • Package-based
  • All-inclusive
  • Stress-free planning
  • Courthouse wedding alternative
  • On-site officiants and celebrants
  • LGBTQIA+ friendly
  • Open to all love stories

I started The Elopery because I went looking for a place like it and could not find one.

Events are my thing.

I have spent years planning them, running them and getting people into the same room for something that matters. I love the run of show. I love the details nobody notices until they are missing.

Then I planned my own wedding.

Every venue I fell for in the DMV came with a price tag that meant starting married life in debt.

I wanted beautiful.I wanted my people there.I did not want a loan.

So I am building the place I wished existed.

The Elopery is an intimate venue for elopements and micro celebrations in the neighborhood I call home, Alexandria. I think this corner of Alexandria deserves a room made for small guest lists and big moments.

A lover of all things extra.

Luxe details, a proper toast, any excuse to bring people together. I also think you should be able to have all of that and still afford the honeymoon.

Where the rest of my heart goes.

By day I work as a consultant, and I run a nonprofit that supports diverse perspectives in psychology. The Elopery is where the rest of my heart goes. I founded it in 2026, and I am working on bringing it to life for celebrations in 2027.
